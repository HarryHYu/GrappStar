## 1. What the frame is now (BASE_SPEC v1.2.x, D23)

- **Motor end.** Motor plate `frame_cheek_r` at y -10..0 (its front face is the spec's y = 0). A window r 88 takes
  the **puck adapter** `frame_motor_adapter` (PPA-CF): motor slots from `params.WIPER` (+-4 mm), M6 heads from the
  front, a rear lip (r 97, y -16..-10) keys it, 8 x M3 from the front at r 92.5 into lip inserts (only those with both
  plate and lip material are used, at least 4 checked), 2 inserts on the front face for cad-drum's SW_INDEX block.
  The wiper boss face is at y -10, so the shaft flats reach the coupler at y 0.5. Puck and plate are carved round the
  reel cup and capstan that pass behind the plate below the axis.
- **Support.** A bridge behind the puck lip carries the plate to `frame_motor_leg` in the free corner between the reel
  cup and the bay (x 78..108, y -55..-30), printed upright on a two-sided gusseted foot (4 x M3 into the deck). A can
  stay `frame_can_stay` from the plate's rear face straps the upright motor can.
- **Gear guards.** `frame_guard_hood` (over the reel and bus gears, hung from the plate front face) and
  `frame_guard_front` (between the drum gears and the bay), carved round every sweep.
- **Deck.** Dovetailed tiles, target footprint plus every foot of every subsystem; an insert under every frame foot
  hole and every round M3 hole found in another subsystem's foot; seams placed as far as possible from holes; rover
  holes never under a foot; 1:1 drill template PDF + CSV.
- **Electronics bay** 124 x 124 in the params region, USB to +x (outward), motor and sensor exits to -x clear of the
  leg, perfboard for the dividers and buses. **Rover kit**: Kiyo X edge-plate mount (tilted to the params pitch),
  RC832 holder, cable anchors.

## 2. Decisions I made (Harry away)

- Read BASE_SPEC section 2 literally (y = 0 is the adapter plate front face): the frame's motor interface moved to
  y <= 0 at 14:05 after review round 3 showed the drum stack and the SW_INDEX mount assume it.
- Frame spring guards dropped: cad-launch owns the D33 spring guard.
- The adapter became a puck in a plate window (not a standoff hub on a cheek): the only way to hold the motor at
  y -10 with the coupler, index block and reel parts all in front of y 0.
- Everything the frame owns near other subsystems is carved from their swept volumes at build time, so the frame
  follows when they move. Cost: the frame build loads the other subsystems (about 35 to 80 s).
