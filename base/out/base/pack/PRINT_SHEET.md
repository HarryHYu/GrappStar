## Recommended settings

### PLA
- 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers
- 4 walls, 5 top and 5 bottom layers
- 40 % gyroid infill
- Nozzle 220 C, bed 55 to 60 C, textured PEI plate
- Door open or top vented

### PPA-CF
- Hardened steel nozzle (0.4 or 0.6 mm), never a brass nozzle
- 0.2 mm layers
- 6 walls, 6 top and 6 bottom layers
- 60 % gyroid infill
- Dry the filament first (80 C for 8 h or more) and print from a dry box
- Enclosure closed, chamber heated if the printer can
- Nozzle 290 to 320 C, bed 100 to 110 C (check the spool label)
