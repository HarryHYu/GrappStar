## What fails or has no margin, and the fix

1. Drill deploy torque. The augers are radial on the pods, so they sweep sideways through the soil. Sweeping both without spinning needs 0.43 N m in dry sand, 3.757 N m in loam, 26.761 N m in firm clay against the deploy worm's 0.207 N m stall. They only go in as a mill (drills spinning, worm at stall): sand 10 s, loam 91 s, clay never. Fix: milling deploy in firmware (dart-fw: worm pulsed at stall, drills full, deploy stall is not the end stop, time caps). A 3:1 stage would cut the loam time by 3 but does not fit under the bay roof (dart-cad).
2. Holding with the v31 deploy horns and kick rest: the tow turns the pods further down, the horns take it, so the worm no longer caps the holding (same result for a worm holding 0.3, 0.6 or 1.2 N m). The horns must carry 4.344 N m for both pods (2.2 N m per pod) at the loam holding; dart-cad to check the horn and hull_top at that moment with a factor 1.5. Kick rest 0.654 N m.
3. Drill torque (d13 augers): install 0.065 to 0.092 N m in loam, unscrew about 0.12 N m against 0.182 N m stall (margin 1.5). The drills can still unscrew in loam up to a cohesion of about 8 kPa; firm clay needs 0.463 to 0.673 N m: it cannot install. State 'loam and softer' in the docs. If the pull test finds stiffer ground, 50 rpm drill motors (same body, twice the torque) are the next step.
4. Motor heating while milling loam (R_th 40 K/W assumed): drills 0.18 A each for 94 s, +19 K; deploy worm at stall +32 K. Acceptable in the PLA pod (softens near 60 C) from a 25 C start; firmware: cap a milling deploy at 150 s, then rest 60 s.
5. Loam holding depends mostly on its cohesion: c = 2 kPa: 25.1 N, 5 kPa: 51.1 N, 10 kPa: 94.6 N, 20 kPa: 181.6 N. The pull test (DART_PULLTEST.md) sets this number; until then use the 5 kPa row.
6. Tow limits. Holding at 3 m: loam 51.1 N (above the 40 N first tow, just under the 55 N cap), dry sand 8.3 N (both tow values drag it). Fix (base/Jetson): first tow at 15 N, then 5 N steps with a 3 s hold, stop when the dart reports motion (pitch change over 2 deg); never above the pull-test 5 mm force / 1.5.
7. Without the foot the loam holding drops from 51.1 to 26.4 N (nose lift); a close rover (1 m, line 21.1 deg) gives 50.0 N. Keep the foot; the line angle matters little with the foot planted.
8. Landing roll. On hard ground the folded dart leans about 42 deg on a flank (no belly-down rest state: CG 6.8 mm above the axis). Swinging the pods down first (drills off) levels it from every leaning state by a pod angle of 18 deg (quasi-static; the tips touch the ground at 16.3 deg). Fix (fw): if |roll| is 10 to 60 deg, pods to 20 deg first, re-read roll, then kick and plant, then the milling deploy. At 30 deg roll the shallow auger would reach only 22.2 mm deep (of 45.7). On its side or upside down the legs cannot right it: report it, reel in, fire again.
9. Hard landings (rigid-body upper bound). Couplers (PPA-CF): soft soil 19.4 MPa (PPA-CF 80 MPa: margin 4.1), gravel 73.7 MPa (PPA-CF 80 MPa: margin 1.1), hard ground 149.6 MPa (PPA-CF 80 MPa: margin 0.5); cells 336.0 N into the front disc on hard ground. Do not fire at concrete, rock or packed gravel. (A crushable nose face would cap a hard hit at about 179 g; not in Harry's parts.)
10. Flight at 7 m/s: nose first only because of the tether (0.25 N brake, about 0.37 N at the dart); with 0, 8 or 15 g ballast the result is the same, and without the tether it tumbles with any of them. v31 has no ballast (agreed). The reel must never let the line go slack in flight.
11. Landing detector. minFlightMs 400 was longer than the shortest flight (0.37 s at 6 deg): 250 ms (dart-fw).
12. Kick and foot. Swing 0.0306 N m of 0.207 (margin 6.8). Planting in loam: the foot moves on an arc, so its last degrees push it sideways: 0.287 N m at 201.75 deg against 0.207 N m (passive, foot not spinning); the worm gets to about 198 deg on its own and the spinning foot (install 0.059 N m) mills the rest: pulse the kick at stall while the foot runs, like the deploy. Bench-check the foot motor: its measured currents imply 0.738 N m, 3.5 times the other worms.
