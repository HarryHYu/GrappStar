## What fails or has no margin, and the fix

1. Drill deploy torque. The augers are radial on the pods, so they sweep sideways through the soil. Sweeping both without spinning needs 0.56 N m in dry sand, 3.786 N m in loam, 26.0 N m in firm clay against the deploy worm's 0.207 N m stall. They only go in as a mill (drills spinning, worm at stall): sand 10.1 s, loam 68.2 s, clay never. Fix: 3:1 spur stage on the deploy axle (dart-cad), milling deploy in firmware (dart-fw).
2. Holding is capped by the deploy worm. Under tow the pods turn back through the worm; with an assumed 0.6 N m holding strength loam holds 23.1 N (rover 3 m) where the soil alone would give 53.6 N. Fix: the same 3:1 stage (worm sees a third), bench test B2 to measure the real strength.
3. Drill torque. Install torque 0.088 to 0.169 N m in loam, unscrew about 0.22 N m, against 0.182 N m stall: release can fail in loam (margin 0.8). Firm clay needs 0.621 to 1.228 N m: cannot install. Fix: auger d17 to d13 (install torque about half; in loam the holding is the same because the cohesion term 9 c D only acts below 1.5 D, which a thinner auger reaches sooner) and 50 rpm drill motors (same body, twice the torque).
4. Motor heating while milling loam (R_th 40 K/W assumed): drills 0.304 A each for 68 s, about +45 K in the PLA pod (softens near 60 C); with the 3:1 stage and d13: 0.179 A, +8 K. Deploy worm +26 K now.
5. With the 3:1 deploy stage and d13 augers alone: loam 38.9 N, deploy 29 s, drill margin 1.53. With 50 rpm drills as well: sand 9.0 N, loam 38.9 N, deploy 59 s in loam, drill margin 3.05 (now 0.83). Firm clay stays out of reach (drill margin 0.42): state 'loam and softer' in the docs.
6. Loam holding depends mostly on its cohesion: c = 2 kPa: 17.3 N now, 25.5 N fixed, 5 kPa: 23.1 N now, 38.9 N fixed, 10 kPa: 32.3 N now, 52.7 N fixed, 20 kPa: 48.5 N now, 73.6 N fixed. The pull test (DART_PULLTEST.md) sets this number; until then use the 5 kPa row.
7. Tow limits. Loam holds 23.1 N now (38.9 N with the fixes) and dry sand about 10.3 N: the 40 N first tow and the 55 N cap drag the dart in both. Fix (base/Jetson): first tow at 15 N, then 5 N steps with a 3 s hold, stop on a dart pitch change over 2 deg or IMU motion; never above the pull-test 5 mm force / 1.5.
8. Without the foot the loam holding drops from 23.1 to 12.3 N (nose lift); a close rover (1 m, line 21.1 deg) gives 18.7 N. Keep the foot; prefer the rover 3 to 5 m away.
9. Landing roll. On hard ground the folded dart leans about 38 deg on a flank. Deploying the pods to about 24 deg levels it (quasi-static check), but the kick runs first today. Fix (fw): if |roll| is 12 to 60 deg, swing the pods to 25 deg first (drills off), then kick and plant, then the full milling deploy. At 30 deg roll the shallow auger reaches only 20.4 mm deep (of 44.8). On its side or upside down nothing the legs do rights it: report it and reel in for another shot. Optional (dart-cad): a 16 mm wide flat on the belly bottom (1.6 mm deep) makes belly down stable for about 17 deg, if the cradle rings allow it.
10. Hard landings. Gravel: coupler bending 69.3 MPa (PLA 45 MPa: margin 0.6); hard ground 153.8 MPa (PLA 45 MPa: margin 0.3), cells 346.0 N into the front disc. Fix: couplers d7 to d9 (PPA-CF if the 3:1 stage is fitted), and a 5 mm crushable nose face (TPU 85A lattice or EVA, plateau about 1.37 MPa) that caps a hard-ground hit at about 188 g; do not fire at concrete or rock.
11. Ballast. At 7 m/s it does almost nothing: the tether keeps the dart nose first with 0, 8 or 15 g, and without the tether it tumbles with any of them. Fix: drop it (saves 15 g, about +0.15 m/s muzzle and 5 percent lower landing loads) or keep at most 5 g as trim; the reel must never let the line go slack in flight.
12. Landing detector. minFlightMs 400 is longer than the shortest flight (0.37 s at 6 deg): use 250 ms (dart-fw).
13. Kick and foot: fine. Swing 0.0324 N m of 0.207 (margin 6.4); planting needs about 0.223 N m in loam at the last degrees (foot spinning); foot install 0.114 N m in loam. Bench-check the foot motor: its measured currents imply 0.738 N m, 3.5 times the other worms.
