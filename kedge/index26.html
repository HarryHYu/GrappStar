How the legs move

Camera: Caddx Ant on the centreline in the nose, looking out a Ø9.8 hole in the Ø22 flat tip. The nose is otherwise solid: no slot, nothing passes through it.
Kick leg, fully OUTSIDE the hull: the self-locking worm N20 lies in a saddle printed on the hull top at x 24–60, worm box at the nose end. Its D output shaft points sideways (+y) and IS the pivot: the leg hub sits on it directly, 1:1, no gears, and a printed stub on the hub's other side runs in an ear fin on the skin. Ø8 tube pole 85 long, foot housing on the end. Folded the pole lies along the hull top at y=12 (beside the saddle). The worm swings it 0–200° over the nose, outboard of the lens; at 180° the foot auger tip touches the ground, by 200° the housing is on the ground and the auger is 38 mm in. Self-locking: holds unpowered.
Foot drill, fixed: a right-angle worm N20 in line with the pole inside the housing, output shaft across the pole, Ø14 × 40 auger on it. Up when folded, down when deployed.
Drill pair: Ø3 through-axle at x=56, z=+5, right behind the nose. Biax worm N20 off-centre on it (y −16..−6), body forward. Legs keyed on the axle D ends, swing 0–38°: pod nose stops 1 mm above the ground, Ø14 × 54 auger 38 mm in, raked tailward (toward the tether pull).
Drills: the measured 12 × 10 × 24.3 motor drops into the rounded pod; its own M3 × 56 shaft runs out through a tapered nose with two 693ZZ and the full-length printed auger threads on; nyloc in the tip hub clamps it.
Inside, nose to tail: camera, then the deploy N20 body with a 3-shelf rack beside it (MP1584 on the floor, MP1584, INA219), the axle, the 4-deck tray (DRV + XIAO, DRV + DRV, VTX + BMS stick, BNO085), the two cells side by side, tail cap. Nothing inside moves.
Hull bottom half is a plain Ø42 cylinder end to end.
