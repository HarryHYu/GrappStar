TEST BUILD: everything printed, nothing bought except the motor. No one way bearings yet: three printed bushings sit where they go, the gears spin free on the axle.
Every pin is printed with a head on one end and a groove on the other; a printed C clip snaps into the groove. 6 clips, 3 bushings.

ASSEMBLY
  1 Push a bushing into the drive gear, one into the pinion, one into the pedestal pocket from the inside face.
  2 Rail body: big gear between the two bracket bosses, gear pin in from the minus side, clip on the end.
  3 Crank gear: crank pin in from the back of the gear (head on the back), rod eye over it, clip. Stub into the pedestal bushing from the inside, clip on the outside. Other rod eye over the lug pin under the rail bracket, clip.
  4 Axle: in from the plus side, head out. It goes through the plus leg, drive gear, both bracket pivot bosses, pinion, minus leg. Clip on the minus end. Drive gear must mesh the big gear, pinion must mesh the crank gear.
  5 Motor on the plus leg plate, shaft into the D socket, 6 x M3.
  6 Carriage into the barrel from the breech, V ridges in the rail grooves, arm through the slit. Springs from the muzzle posts to the carriage pegs. Dart in at the muzzle.

CLIPS: the opening is 0.9 mm narrower than the groove core, push straight on, it snaps. Pull off with a flat blade. If one is too tight, scale it 102% in the slicer.
LATER: swap the three bushings for HF1216 one way bearings (same 18 x 12 x 16 size) and it becomes the working launcher.

PRINT: 0.2 mm layers. Gears, pins, clips, rod, bushings 100% infill. Rail body and frame 4 walls 30%. Supports where noted.
