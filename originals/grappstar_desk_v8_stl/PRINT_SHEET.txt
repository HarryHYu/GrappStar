ASSEMBLY: the axle is hex below the top leg. Drop the axle down through the top leg, upper bracket boss, gear (hex bore, slides on from the hex end), lower boss and bottom leg; crank on top. Rail body swings freely on the axle: hold the pitch by hand.
Slide the carriage cup in through the open back of the barrel, V ridges into the rail grooves, arm through the slit. Two small band loops from the muzzle posts to the carriage pegs.
CYCLE: dart in at the muzzle, tail first. Hold the pitch. Turn the crank: the gear teeth drag the rack back 150 mm, then the gap lets go and the bands fire the carriage. One turn per shot.
Print: gear, rack and carriage solid; axle 100% infill; rail body and frame 4 walls, 20% infill.
