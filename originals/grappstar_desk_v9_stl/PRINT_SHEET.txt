BOUGHT PARTS
  1 x 37 mm 12 V gearmotor, 6 mm D shaft, ~100 rpm (JGB37-520 type), 6 x M3 screws for its face
  3 x HF1216 one way needle bearing (12 x 18 x 16 mm): A in the drive gear, B in the pinion, C in the pedestal. Press fit, bores are 18.3 mm
  12 mm steel rod, cut to: axle (through both legs, D socket head bonded on the plus end) 98 mm, big gear stub (pressed in the two bracket bosses) 40 mm, crank gear stub (bonded in the crank gear, sits in bearing C) 28 mm, crank pin (pressed through the crank gear) 24 mm, lug pin (pressed in the rail lug boss) 12 mm
  2 x extension spring about 10 mm OD, 40 mm free length, hooks each end (stretch 150 mm). Loop over the muzzle posts and the carriage pegs
  A little cyanoacrylate for the D socket head and the crank gear stub

ONE WAY BEARING DIRECTIONS (check before pressing, they only go one way)
  A (drive gear): must lock when the axle turns in the cocking direction and run free the other way
  B (pinion): the opposite of A: locks on the backward turn, free on the cocking turn
  C (pedestal): the crank gear turns freely clockwise (seen from the minus side, the direction backward motor turns drive it) and locks the other way

ASSEMBLY
  1 Press bearing A into the drive gear hub, B into the pinion, C into the pedestal pocket (from the inside face).
  2 Rail body: press the big gear stub through the two bracket bosses with the big gear on it between them (gear bore is a running fit). Press the lug pin into the lug boss, pointing down toward the gears.
  3 Crank gear: bond the crank stub into the hub and press the crank pin through its bore. Slip the rod eye over the crank pin, then push the stub into bearing C in the pedestal. Swing the rod up and drop its other eye over the lug pin.
  4 Axle: slide it in from the minus side through the minus leg, pinion (bearing B), the rail bracket pivot bosses, the drive gear (bearing A) and the plus leg, head outside the plus leg. The drive gear must mesh the big gear, the pinion must mesh the crank gear.
  5 Motor on the outside of the plus leg plate, shaft into the D socket, 6 x M3.
  6 Carriage: slide it into the barrel from the breech with the V ridges in the rail grooves and the arm through the slit. Springs from the muzzle posts to the carriage pegs. Dart in at the muzzle.

RUNNING
  Motor forward: cocks and fires, about 1.6 motor turns per shot. Motor backward: raises the pitch (about one backward turn covers 6 to 47 degrees); past the top it rolls over and drops back to 6 degrees. The pitch holds by itself.

PRINT
  Gears, rack/carriage, rod: 100% infill, 0.2 mm layers, flat as noted. Rail body and frame: 4 walls, 30% infill. Supports where noted. PLA is fine; PETG for the gears if you have it.
