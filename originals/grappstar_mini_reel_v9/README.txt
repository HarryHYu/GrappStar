GrappStar mini reel prototype v9 (18 Sep 2026). Same mechanism and dimensions as v8; every rotating joint now on a real axle.
Assembled 148 x 86 x 56 mm. PLA, 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports: every STL is already in its print orientation.
Same parts and positions as the sim.

JOINTS
  Capstan axle: 6 mm D-rod, 47.3 long. The drum is keyed to it (D-bore, push fit). It runs in the capstan bracket's
  6.4 round hole. The crank is keyed to the rear end. The drum's rear flange (0.5 mm in front of the bracket) and the
  crank (0.5 mm behind it) capture the bracket, so the axle can't walk. On the front the axle carries the wrap reel on a
  6.4 round bore, sitting between a 0.8 mm boss on the drum's nose and a 9 mm cap screwed into the axle end
  (M3 x 8 countersunk, 0.2 + 0.3 mm end play). The wrap reel spins free; the cap turns with the drum.
  Spool axle: 6 mm D-rod, 38 long. Spool keyed to it, runs in the reel bracket's 6.4 hole, crank keyed to the rear end,
  bracket captured between the spool's rear boss and the crank with 0.5 mm each side.
  Wrap reel lock: M3 x 16 through the lock bracket into a hole in the wrap reel's rim, 90 deg from the lug.

PRINT LIST (14 pieces)
  base x1             pegs up
  capstan_bracket x1
  drum x1             base flange face down, nose boss up (D-bore is a through hole, no bridging)
  axle_capstan x1     lying down, flat on the bed
  cap x1              flat, countersink up
  wrap_reel x1        rear face down, lug up (through bore, no bridging)
  lock_bracket x1
  crank x2
  reel_bracket x1
  spool x1            front flange face down, rear boss up
  axle_spool x1       lying down, flat on the bed
  eye_post x2         guide at the base end, exit eye in front of the wrap reel

HARDWARE: M3 x 8 countersunk (cap), M3 x 16 (lock bolt), line 1 mm or thinner.

ASSEMBLY (see mini_assembled.png)
  1. Modules onto base pegs: reel bracket back-left, guide post beside it, capstan bracket behind the drum,
     lock bracket front-right of the drum, exit eye post further right.
  2. Capstan: push the drum onto the axle, flat to flat, rear flange 2.5 mm from the axle's rear end... easier: slide the
     axle through the bracket from the front, push the drum on from the front until its flange is 0.5 mm off the bracket,
     push the crank onto the rear end. Then the wrap reel over the front, lug at the top, then the cap and M3 x 8.
  3. Spool: axle through the reel bracket from the front, spool on until its boss is 0.5 mm off the bracket, crank on the back.
  4. M3 x 16 into the lock bracket. Screwed in it enters the wrap reel's rim hole; backed out 6 mm the reel is free.
  5. Line: tie to spool, over the spool top, through the guide, over the drum, through the lug on the wrap reel,
     through the exit eye, out. Tie the far end to something.

HAND TEST
  Wrap:   lock out. Far end taut. Turn the wrap reel 4 turns by its rim; the spool pays out; turns lay on the capstan.
  Tow:    lock in. Drum crank while winding the spool crank. Line in through the lug, over the wrap reel rim onto the
          capstan, round the helix to the base, off to the spool.
  Unwrap: lock out, wrap reel 4 turns back while winding the spool.

FITS: axle 6.0 D in 6.2 D bores (push), 6.4 round holes (free); base pegs 4.6 in 4.84 (press); cranks 6.4 D (push).
If a D-bore is tight after printing, a few strokes with a file on the axle flat. CA the drum to the axle if it slips.
