KEDGE PROOF-OF-CONCEPT — printable, electronics-free, 55% desk scale
=====================================================================
Joints are FULL-SIZE (pins O5, tongues 4.2, slots 4.8) so the hinges
feel real even though the model is scaled to fit a 150mm printer.

PRINT (PLA or PETG, 0.2mm, 3 walls, 20-25%):
  poc_body.stl       x1  VERTICAL, nose up (143mm tall). Brim.
                         Small supports under clevis bridge + bosses.
  poc_leg_drill.stl  x2  vertical, tongue on bed, auger tip up. No supports.
  poc_leg_kick.stl   x1  lying flat on its side. Brim; small support at elbow.
  poc_snap_pin.stl   x3  head down, flat. Print 4 (one spare).

ASSEMBLE (no tools):
  1. Drill legs: hold tongue between the side bosses, push a snap pin
     from OUTSIDE through boss + tongue until the slotted tip clicks
     past the far side. Leg swings on the pin. Repeat other side.
  2. Kick leg: tongue into the top clevis slot, snap pin through.
  3. Too tight? Ream bores with a 5mm drill bit twisted by hand.

PLAY CHECKLIST (this is the design, in your hand):
  [] Fold all three legs rearward — dart shape, legs are the fins
  [] Deploy the pair 45 deg toward the tail eye — the anchor rake
  [] Sweep the kick right over the nose — the over-the-top arc
  [] Set it on the table hull-down, kick swept flush: the DOGLEG puts
     the pole FLAT on the table while the body sits flat. That crook
     is the flush law: drop = pivot height - hull R + pole R.
  [] Pull the eye horizontally with legs "planted" — feel the rake
     convert pull into dig-in. That's the whole machine.

Pivots sit higher + wider per design review 06-08 (wider chord = wider
leg stance). Full-scale motorised build uses the same joint geometry.
