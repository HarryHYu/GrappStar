# Environment asset credits

All third-party assets used by `render/env.py` are CC0 (public domain): free for any use, no attribution
required. They are listed here anyway. They are not committed: `render/assets/fetch_env.py` downloads them to
`render/assets/cache/` (gitignored). Without them `env.py` still builds, with procedural fallback materials and
a physical sky instead of the HDRI.

| Weathered timber fence posts, strainers, stays | Rough Wood (2K JPG) | https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood | Rob Tuytel | CC0 |

Poly Haven texture files follow the pattern
`https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/<id>/<id>_<map>_2k.jpg` (map = diff, nor_gl, arm);
`fetch_env.py` resolves them through `https://api.polyhaven.com/files/<id>`.

Licence: Poly Haven https://polyhaven.com/license (CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Everything else in the environment is generated by `env.py` itself: terrain, the dry grass (blades, tussocks,
wild oat / barley grass / ryegrass seed heads), gum trees (trunks, branches, pendulous leaf clusters, hanging
bark), ground litter (gum leaves, bark strips, twigs, sticks, rocks, dried manure), the timber post and wire
fence with star pickets, caps and sighter tape, the tube gate, trough, fallen limbs, shed, round bales, the
sulphur-crested cockatoos and their animation, and the procedural shaders (hay, galvanised steel, zincalume,
plumage, rock, manure, bark strips).
