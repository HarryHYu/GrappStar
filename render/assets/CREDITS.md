# Environment asset credits

All third-party assets used by `render/env.py` are CC0 (public domain): free for any use, no attribution
required. They are listed here anyway. They are not committed: `render/assets/fetch_env.py` downloads them to
`render/assets/cache/` (gitignored). Without them `env.py` still builds, with procedural fallback materials and
a physical sky instead of the HDRI.

Poly Haven texture files follow the pattern
`https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/<id>/<id>_<map>_2k.jpg` (map = diff, nor_gl, arm);
`fetch_env.py` resolves them through `https://api.polyhaven.com/files/<id>`.

Licences: Poly Haven https://polyhaven.com/license, ambientCG https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ (both CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Everything else in the environment (terrain, grass blades, clover, plantain, dandelion, buttercups, seed heads,
fencing, gate, logs, stump, hay bale, trough, and the procedural end-grain, hay, galvanised and water shaders) is
generated by `env.py` itself.
