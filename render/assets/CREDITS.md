# Environment asset credits

All third-party assets used by `render/env.py` are CC0 (public domain): free for any use, no attribution
required. They are listed here anyway. They are not committed: `render/assets/fetch_env.py` downloads them to
`render/assets/cache/` (gitignored). Without them `env.py` still builds, with procedural fallback materials and
a physical sky instead of the HDRI.

| Use in the scene | Asset | Source / URL | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sky, ambient light, distant trees (4K .hdr; sun lamp matched to its sun: elevation 52 deg, 5.1 W/m2 measured) | Pretoria Gardens | https://polyhaven.com/a/pretoria_gardens (file: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/4k/pretoria_gardens_4k.hdr) | Dimitrios Savva (photography), Jarod Guest (processing) | CC0 |
| Mown lawn sward under the 3D grass blades | Grass 004 (2K JPG: Color, NormalGL, Roughness) | https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004 (file: https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Grass004_2K-JPG.zip) | ambientCG (Lennart Demes) | CC0 |
| Small bare soil spots in the lawn | Dry Ground Rocks (2K JPG: diff, nor_gl, arm) | https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_rocks | Rob Tuytel | CC0 |
| Tree trunks and branches (relief, re-toned), dead trees, stump, sticks | Bark Bluegum (2K JPG) | https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_bluegum | Dimitrios Savva | CC0 |
| Weathered post-and-rail fence, gate posts, shelter posts | Rough Wood (2K JPG) | https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood | Rob Tuytel | CC0 |

Poly Haven texture files follow the pattern
`https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/<id>/<id>_<map>_2k.jpg` (map = diff, nor_gl, arm);
`fetch_env.py` resolves them through `https://api.polyhaven.com/files/<id>`.

Licences: Poly Haven https://polyhaven.com/license, ambientCG https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ (both CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Everything else in the environment is generated by `env.py` itself, laid out after Harry's photos of his yard:
terrain, the mown lawn blades, trees (eucalypts, bushy trees, Norfolk Island pine, dead and bare trees, shrubs) and
their foliage, the post-and-rail fence, mesh gate, electric tape line with tread-in posts and star pickets, stump,
sandstone boulders, sacks, shelter, sparse litter, the sulphur-crested cockatoo and its walk / peck / flight
animation, and the procedural shaders (sandstone, sacks, end grain, steel, plumage, rock).
